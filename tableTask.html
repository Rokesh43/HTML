Chicken biriyani

Perp Time : 20 Mins

Cook Time :45 Mins

Steps

  1. Wash and soak the rice for 20–30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil and ghee in a pot.
  3. Add whole spices (bay leaf, cinnamon, cloves, cardamom)
  4. Add chicken / mutton / vegetables and sauté for a few minutes
  5. Close the lid and cook on low flame (dum) until rice becomes soft.

Ingredient

Ingredient Quantity
Basmati Rice 2 cup
Chicken 1kg
Tomato 150g
Onion
salt required
